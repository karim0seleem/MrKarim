🧮 Essential Digital SAT Desmos Hacks & Golden Rules (Eng. Karim Seleem)

Use "~" (tilde) for Instant Regression in Bluebook Desmos!

📈 1. Linear & Quadratic Regression

y₁ ~ m x₁ + b y₁ ~ a x₁² + b x₁ + c

Given 2 points (linear) or 3 points (quadratic), enter them into a Desmos table (x₁, y₁) and type the regression formula to get the exact equation in seconds.

🎯 2. Equivalent Expressions Slider Hack

f(x) = Given Expression g(x) = Answer Choice

Graph the question expression and each choice—matching curves overlap 100%! Or set a constant a = 2.3 and compare numeric outputs.

⚖️ 3. Systems of Equations & Solutions

a₁/a₂ = b₁/b₂ ≠ c₁/c₂ (No Sol) a₁/a₂ = b₁/b₂ = c₁/c₂ (Infinite)

Type both equations directly into Desmos—no need to isolate y! Click the intersection dot for (x, y).

🌀 4. Quadratics, Vertex & Discriminant

Δ = b² - 4ac Sum = -b/a | Product = c/a

Δ > 0: 2 real solutions · Δ = 0: 1 real solution (tangent to x-axis) · Δ < 0: 0 real solutions.

⭕ 5. Circle Standard Equation

(x - h)² + (y - k)² = r²

Center is (h, k) and radius is r. You can plot the circle directly in Desmos and click extreme top/bottom/left/right points!

📊 6. Percent & Exponential Growth

f(t) = a · (1 ± r)^(t/k)

Increase by p% → multiply by (1 + p/100). Decrease by p% → multiply by (1 - p/100).
